# Mood notes

One `## mood` section per mood word used in the song catalog. Metadata lines are parsed.

## happy
- aliases: joyful, cheerful, good mood, positive
- related_moods: euphoric, romantic
- related_genres: pop, indie pop, reggae
- energy: 0.60-0.90
- keywords: smile, sunny, bright, celebrate, feel good, morning
Happy music is bright and major-key with moderate to high energy. Euphoric tracks are the same feeling turned up.

## chill
- aliases: relaxing, laid back, mellow, calm, easygoing
- related_moods: relaxed, focused
- related_genres: lofi, ambient, jazz
- energy: 0.20-0.50
- keywords: unwind, rest, evening, quiet, soft, cozy, lazy, background, night
Chill music keeps tempo and energy low so it can sit in the background. Relaxed and focused are its closest moods.

## focused
- aliases: concentration, productive, deep work, zone
- related_moods: chill, relaxed
- related_genres: lofi, classical, ambient
- energy: 0.25-0.50
- keywords: study, studying, work, coding, homework, concentrate, reading, exam, deadline
Focused listening needs steady, predictable, mostly instrumental music with no sudden changes.

## relaxed
- aliases: easy, unwinding, restful, peaceful
- related_moods: chill, romantic
- related_genres: jazz, reggae, ambient
- energy: 0.25-0.60
- keywords: weekend, coffee, sunday, beach, dinner, calm, slow
Relaxed music is easygoing and warm and a little more rhythmic than chill.

## intense
- aliases: powerful, hard, pumped, fierce
- related_moods: angry, euphoric, confident
- related_genres: rock, metal, edm
- energy: 0.80-1.00
- keywords: workout, gym, running, lifting, adrenaline, fast, training, race
Intense music is high in energy and tempo, good for workouts and anything physical.

## angry
- aliases: furious, rage, aggressive, mad
- related_moods: intense, confident
- related_genres: metal, rock, hip hop
- energy: 0.85-1.00
- keywords: rage, frustration, vent, heavy, loud, fury
Angry music channels frustration through distortion and speed, and sits at the extreme top of the energy scale.

## moody
- aliases: brooding, dark, atmospheric, noir
- related_moods: melancholy, intense
- related_genres: synthwave, ambient, r&b
- energy: 0.50-0.80
- keywords: night, drive, midnight, city, rain, neon, shadows
Moody music is dark but still rhythmic, often with minor-key synthesizers.

## romantic
- aliases: love, date night, sensual, intimate
- related_moods: relaxed, happy
- related_genres: r&b, jazz, pop
- energy: 0.35-0.70
- keywords: date, valentine, dinner, slow dance, couple, anniversary, candlelight
Romantic music is warm and mid-tempo with a smooth vocal or instrumental lead.

## confident
- aliases: swagger, boss, empowered, bold
- related_moods: intense, happy
- related_genres: hip hop, rock, r&b
- energy: 0.60-0.90
- keywords: motivation, hype, walk, strut, power, interview, win
Confident music is bold and rhythmic, with a strong beat that gives a lift before a big moment.

## euphoric
- aliases: ecstatic, elated, uplifted, blissful
- related_moods: happy, intense
- related_genres: edm, pop, synthwave
- energy: 0.80-1.00
- keywords: festival, rave, drop, dance, peak, celebration, club
Euphoric music builds toward a release, and is the high-energy relative of happy.

## nostalgic
- aliases: reminiscing, throwback, memories, homesick
- related_moods: wistful, melancholy
- related_genres: country, folk, indie pop
- energy: 0.35-0.65
- keywords: childhood, hometown, old days, remember, retro, memories, sunset
Nostalgic music is warm and familiar, with acoustic textures and a mid-tempo pace.

## wistful
- aliases: yearning, bittersweet, longing
- related_moods: nostalgic, melancholy
- related_genres: folk, indie pop, country
- energy: 0.20-0.50
- keywords: autumn, missing, letters, longing, window, gentle
Wistful music is gentle and bittersweet, between happy and sad.

## melancholy
- aliases: pensive, gloomy, reflective, somber
- related_moods: sad, wistful
- related_genres: classical, blues, ambient
- energy: 0.10-0.45
- keywords: rain, rainy, reflect, quiet, grey, introspective, slow
Melancholy music is slow and reflective, usually in a minor key with sparse instrumentation.

## sad
- aliases: heartbroken, down, blue, crying, lonely
- related_moods: melancholy, wistful
- related_genres: blues, folk, classical
- energy: 0.20-0.55
- keywords: breakup, heartbreak, tears, lonely, cry, grief, hurt
Sad music is slow and low in energy. High-energy sad songs are rare, so a sad request at a very high energy level is a contradictory one.
