# Genre notes

Hand-written notes used by the retriever. One `## genre` section per genre.
Metadata lines (`- key: value`) are parsed; the paragraph after them is the text the
recommender quotes in its explanations.

## pop
- aliases: pop music, top 40, mainstream
- related_genres: indie pop, edm
- related_moods: happy, romantic, euphoric
- energy: 0.60-0.95
- keywords: catchy, radio, singalong, party, dance, workout, upbeat, bright
Pop is built around catchy hooks and polished production, and it bends easily toward dance or indie sounds. Fans of pop usually tolerate neighbouring indie pop and EDM.

## indie pop
- aliases: indie, bedroom pop
- related_genres: pop, folk
- related_moods: happy, wistful, nostalgic
- energy: 0.50-0.80
- keywords: quirky, sunny, jangly, summer, roadtrip, window, lighthearted
Indie pop keeps pop melodies but trades gloss for a handmade feel, so it is the closest relative of mainstream pop and a soft landing for folk listeners.

## lofi
- aliases: lo-fi, lofi hip hop, chillhop
- related_genres: ambient, jazz, classical
- related_moods: chill, focused, relaxed
- energy: 0.25-0.50
- keywords: study, studying, homework, focus, coding, work, late night, night, rain, background, cozy, concentrate, reading
Lofi is low-tempo, mostly instrumental and warm, which is why it dominates study and coding playlists. Ambient, jazz and classical share the same quiet, unobtrusive texture.

## rock
- aliases: rock music, guitar music, alt rock
- related_genres: metal, blues
- related_moods: intense, angry, confident
- energy: 0.65-0.95
- keywords: guitar, drums, riff, loud, stadium, driving, road, storm
Rock is guitar-driven and loud. When there are few rock songs to choose from, metal and blues are the nearest neighbours in energy and attitude.

## metal
- aliases: heavy metal, hard rock
- related_genres: rock, edm
- related_moods: angry, intense
- energy: 0.85-1.00
- keywords: heavy, brutal, aggressive, distortion, headbang, fury, gym
Metal sits at the top of the energy scale with distorted guitars and fast drums. Rock is its gentlest neighbour; EDM matches the raw intensity with synthesizers instead of guitars.

## ambient
- aliases: atmospheric, soundscape, drone
- related_genres: lofi, classical
- related_moods: chill, relaxed, melancholy
- energy: 0.10-0.35
- keywords: space, floating, meditate, meditation, sleep, calm, drift, slow, dreamy, quiet
Ambient favours texture over rhythm. It suits sleep, meditation and background listening, and sits next to lofi and classical.

## jazz
- aliases: jazzy, bebop, smooth jazz
- related_genres: lofi, blues, r&b
- related_moods: relaxed, romantic, chill
- energy: 0.25-0.60
- keywords: coffee, cafe, dinner, evening, saxophone, piano, lounge, sophisticated, brunch
Jazz is improvisational and mellow in its lounge forms. Lofi borrows jazz chords, and blues and R&B share its phrasing.

## synthwave
- aliases: retrowave, outrun, 80s synth
- related_genres: edm, pop
- related_moods: moody, euphoric, confident
- energy: 0.55-0.85
- keywords: neon, night drive, driving, retro, 80s, city, cyberpunk, midnight
Synthwave revives 1980s synthesizer sounds and works well for night driving. EDM and synth-heavy pop are the closest alternatives.

## hip hop
- aliases: rap, hiphop, trap
- related_genres: r&b, lofi
- related_moods: confident, angry, intense
- energy: 0.60-0.90
- keywords: beats, bars, swagger, hustle, street, flow, concrete
Hip hop is rhythm and lyric first. R&B is its melodic cousin, and lofi hip hop is its quiet offshoot.

## r&b
- aliases: rnb, rhythm and blues, soul
- related_genres: jazz, hip hop, pop
- related_moods: romantic, relaxed, confident
- energy: 0.35-0.70
- keywords: smooth, slow jam, date, love, velvet, soulful, intimate
R&B centres on smooth vocals and groove. It is the natural bridge between jazz, hip hop and romantic pop.

## edm
- aliases: electronic, dance music, house, techno
- related_genres: pop, synthwave
- related_moods: euphoric, happy, intense
- energy: 0.80-1.00
- keywords: club, rave, festival, drop, dancing, workout, party, bass
EDM is made for dance floors and festivals, with repeating build-and-drop structures. Pop and synthwave are the closest softer relatives.

## country
- aliases: country music, americana, honky tonk
- related_genres: folk, blues
- related_moods: nostalgic, wistful, sad
- energy: 0.40-0.70
- keywords: truck, road, porch, hometown, dust, small town, guitar, story
Country is story-driven and nostalgic. Folk and blues share its acoustic roots and storytelling.

## classical
- aliases: orchestral, symphony, piano music, baroque
- related_genres: ambient, jazz
- related_moods: melancholy, relaxed, chill
- energy: 0.10-0.40
- keywords: sonata, piano, orchestra, elegant, rain, study, focus, quiet, concentrate
Classical is acoustic and composed. Quiet piano pieces are a classic study companion and sit near ambient and jazz.

## reggae
- aliases: roots reggae, ska, dub
- related_genres: folk, r&b
- related_moods: relaxed, happy, chill
- energy: 0.45-0.70
- keywords: island, beach, sunshine, summer, laid back, offbeat, groove, vacation
Reggae is laid-back and offbeat-driven, ideal for sunny, unhurried listening.

## folk
- aliases: acoustic, singer songwriter, folk music
- related_genres: country, indie pop
- related_moods: wistful, nostalgic, melancholy
- energy: 0.20-0.55
- keywords: acoustic, guitar, campfire, heartfelt, lyrics, autumn, storytelling, gentle
Folk is acoustic and lyric-driven. Country and indie pop are its nearest neighbours.

## blues
- aliases: delta blues, blues rock
- related_genres: jazz, rock, country
- related_moods: sad, melancholy, nostalgic
- energy: 0.30-0.65
- keywords: heartbreak, guitar, slow, soulful, lonely, porch, rainy, delta
Blues is built on expressive guitar and sorrowful lyrics and rarely runs at high energy. Jazz, rock and country grew out of or alongside it.
